How it works

  1. Fill in your details. Your name, platform, and handle — pick Instagram, YouTube, X, or Facebook and we build your profile link for you automatically, so there's nothing to copy-paste or get wrong.
  2. Pick your rank. Enter a bid amount and see the rank it lands you right now, or the other way round — type the rank you want and we'll show the bid needed to take it. Minimum bid is ₹100, and outranking someone (including your own earlier bid) needs at least ₹50 more than their current bid — not just ₹1 more. Ties go to whoever bid first.
  3. Submit and pay. Submitting gives you an entry ID — pay that amount to our UPI ID, then send the payment screenshot on WhatsApp along with your entry ID so it can be matched to your request.
  4. We review by hand. No payment gateway here — the site owner checks your screenshot against their own UPI history and approves it manually, usually within a day. Track the result any time in "Your entries" above.
  5. Live, rejected, or still pending. Approved entries go straight to the leaderboard. If something's off, you'll see the reason under "Your entries" and can fix it with one click via Edit & resubmit — no need to start over. Changed your mind before review? Remove your request any time it's still pending.
  6. Boost or share, any time. Already listed? Bid higher later to climb the board with ⬆ Boost — again, at least ₹50 more than your current bid. Either way, 📤 Share turns your rank (or your bid, before it's even approved) into a shareable card for Instagram/WhatsApp.
Is my spot permanent?

Yes — once approved, your listing stays up. Someone can only outrank you by bidding higher; it never expires on its own.

Why do I need to bid ₹50 more, not just ₹1 more?

Without a floor on it, outranking someone would turn into an endless back-and-forth over single rupees. A ₹50 minimum step means every bid actually moves the board, whether you're topping up your own listing or trying to pass someone else's.

Why was I rejected, and what happens to my money?

This site never touches your payment directly — you only pay after seeing your entry ID, and only we mark it approved after manually checking your screenshot. If we reject a request, no charge should have gone through on our end for it; if you already paid before hearing back, message us on WhatsApp with your entry ID.

Can I change my handle or platform after submitting?

Not on the same entry — a listing is keyed to one platform + handle. Remove the pending request and submit a fresh one instead, or if it's already live, reach out on WhatsApp.

Is "Your entries" tied to my account?

There's no account system on this site. "Your entries" is just a note this browser keeps for itself about what it submitted — clearing your browser data or switching devices means it won't remember, though your listing itself is unaffected either way.
